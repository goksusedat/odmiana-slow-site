Odmiana słów Support

Updated: 2026-05-03

Odmiana słów is an offline Polish noun and verb inflection dictionary.

Contact

For support, feedback, or correction requests, contact odmianaslowapp@gmail.com.

Before Contacting Support

Please include the searched word, the form you expected, your device model, your iOS version, and whether the issue happens offline or online.

Common Checks

  • If search does not find a word, try the base form.
  • If Polish characters are difficult to type, try the non-accented version, for example pracowac.
  • If ads do not appear, check the internet connection. The dictionary lookup itself works offline.

Odmiana słów Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-03

Odmiana słów is a Polish noun and verb inflection dictionary.

Data Stored On Device

The app stores favorite entries, recent searches, selected theme, and selected text size locally on the user's device. This local data is used only to provide app features. It is not sent to an app account or custom backend service.

Dictionary Lookup

The dictionary database is bundled with the app. Core search and inflection lookup work offline and do not require account registration.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device, advertising, diagnostics, app activity, or ad interaction data to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow before requesting ads and provides an advertising privacy options entry point in Settings.

Users can learn more about Google's advertising data practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates Google AdMob for advertising. Google AdMob policies and related information are available at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general language reference tool and is not specifically directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact odmianaslowapp@gmail.com.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when app features, advertising settings, or legal requirements change.
